🔐 Cryptographic Hybrid Encryption

How YouPi Protects Your Payment:

  • Zero plaintext exposure: Intermediate mesh phones only see ciphertext and cannot tamper with amounts or VPAs.
  • RSA-2048 / OAEP-SHA256: The symmetric AES-256 session key is sealed using the banking gateway's public key.
  • AES-GCM Authenticated Cipher: Prevents bit-flipping attacks during mesh packet propagation.
  • SHA-256 Idempotency: Duplicate copies circulating in the mesh are automatically de-duplicated at the bank server.
